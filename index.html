FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Rendry really free?

Yes. The free tier captures full pages as PNG or PDF, handles lazy-loaded images, hides annoying cookie banners/chat widgets, and warns about infinite scroll — all without any server dependencies. Pro adds lossy formats, resolution controls, and the built-in annotation editor.

Does Rendry track my screenshots?

No. We operate on a local-first philosophy. Your captures are kept strictly in browser memory and saved to your local downloads folder. No screenshot data is ever sent to our servers. The free tier makes zero network requests.

How does the annotation editor work?

When you toggle "Open in Editor" in the Pro Features menu, Rendry opens a new local tab containing our design editor right after capture. You can draw vectors, blur personal info, write text overlays, and then copy the resulting canvas directly to your clipboard or download it.

What is ExtensionPay?

ExtensionPay is a secure paywall service built specifically for browser extensions. It manages licenses through Stripe. This allows us to securely process your payments using your checkout email without running a custom database or requiring a password profile.

What is the refund policy?

We provide a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first charge of Pro. Simply email support@rendry.app from your checkout address and we will issue a full refund immediately. Cancellations stop renewals for the next period.

How do I report a bug?

Please open a bug ticket or discussion request in our public repository at github.com/mamaownedus/rendry-site/issues. We actively review all submissions.
