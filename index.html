FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Rendry really free?

Yes. The free tier captures full pages as PNG or PDF, handles lazy-loaded images, hides annoying cookie banners/chat widgets, warns about infinite scroll, and includes the built-in editor (crop, copy, and PNG/PDF export) — all without any server dependencies. Pro adds Smart Redact (auto-hide sensitive details), the Extract panel (copy a page's text, links, tables, and design tokens), element capture, lossy formats, resolution controls, compression presets, and the editor's markup tools (blur, arrows, shapes, text).

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Rendry Pro starts with a 7-day free trial — full Pro access, no card required. Start it with one click from the extension popup or the editor. If Pro isn't for you, do nothing: the trial simply ends and the free tier keeps working forever.

Does Rendry track my screenshots?

No. We operate on a local-first philosophy. Your captures are kept strictly in browser memory and saved to your local downloads folder — on the free tier and Pro alike. No screenshot data is ever sent to our servers. The only network request Rendry ever makes is the license check (your email and subscription status) — never your pages or captures.

How does the annotation editor work?

"Open in Editor" is on by default for everyone (toggle it off in the popup for a one-click download). Right after capture, Rendry opens a new local tab with our editor. Crop, copy to clipboard, and PNG/PDF export are free; the markup tools — blur, arrows, shapes, and text — are Pro. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded.

What is ExtensionPay?

ExtensionPay is a secure paywall service built specifically for browser extensions. It manages licenses through Stripe. This allows us to securely process your payments using your checkout email without running a custom database or requiring a password profile.

What is the refund policy?

We provide a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first charge of Pro. Simply email support@rendry.app from your checkout address and we will issue a full refund immediately. Cancellations stop renewals for the next period.

How do I report a bug?

Please open a bug ticket or discussion request in our public repository at github.com/mamaownedus/rendry-site/issues. We actively review all submissions.

How do I take a full-page screenshot of a web page?

Install Rendry, open the page, and click the Rendry icon — or press Alt+Shift+R. It captures the entire page top to bottom in one click, including lazy-loaded images, and saves it as a PNG or PDF. No scrolling, no stitching, no DevTools. It works the same across Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, Arc, and Comet.

How do I save a full web page as a PDF?

Choose PDF in the Rendry popup before you capture, then click the icon. Rendry saves the entire page as a single clean PDF — including content below the fold — for free. Pro adds multi-page A4/Letter layouts and custom filenames if you need them.

How do I hide sensitive details in a screenshot before sharing it?

Turn on Smart Redact (Pro) in the Rendry popup and it hides sensitive details before you save, then lets you review, undo, or restore each one in the editor — so a bug report or support reply never leaks private information. Everything stays on your machine.

Can I copy the text, links, and tables from a webpage capture?

Yes. Rendry Pro lets you copy clean, ready-to-paste text, a tidy list of links, or a spreadsheet-ready table from any capture via the editor's Extract panel — as Markdown, CSV, or TSV. It's handy for content audits, research, and pasting a page into an AI assistant.

How do I extract a website's colours and fonts?

With Rendry Pro, the Extract panel turns any page's colours, fonts, and spacing into reusable design tokens — exported as CSS variables or JSON. It's bundled into the capture flow and runs locally, so you can grab a site's look without a separate tool or any upload.

How do I screenshot just one element on a webpage?

Choose Capture element in the Rendry popup (Pro), then click the element you want. Rendry captures only that element — including the part scrolled below the fold — instead of the whole page. It's great for grabbing a single component for a spec, moodboard, or bug report.

Does Rendry work in Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera, and Comet?

Yes. Rendry works across Chromium browsers — Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, Arc, and Comet — so you get the same one-click full-page capture, clean-up, and editor everywhere, with the same local-first privacy.
