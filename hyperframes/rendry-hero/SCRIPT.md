# Silent Hero Loop

No narration or audio. The visual action is the script.

1. Rendry opens over the captured Smart Redact demo page.
2. Capture Full Page starts; the viewport auto-scrolls while scan segments accumulate.
3. The stitched capture opens in Rendry's editor.
4. Smart Redact finds and blurs the sensitive sample values.
5. The cursor selects another area and draws a red rectangle.
6. The editor settles, then the composition fades into its loop reset.
