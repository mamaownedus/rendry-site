Changelog

Release history. Newer versions on top.

v1.4.0

June 2026

New in Pro

  • Smart Redact — automatically hide sensitive details like emails and card numbers before you save, and review, undo, or restore any of them right in the editor.
  • Copy text, links & tables — turn any capture into clean, ready-to-paste text, a tidy list of links, or a spreadsheet-ready table.
  • Design tokens — grab a page’s colours, fonts, and spacing in one click.
  • Element capture — capture just the part of the page you want.
  • 7-day free trial — try everything in Pro free for a week.

As always, everything happens right in your browser — your screenshots never leave your machine.

v1.3.0

June 2026

Added

  • The built-in editor is now free for everyone. Captures open in the editor by default — a new Open in Editor toggle in the popup, on by default; switch it off any time for one-click download. Free users get Crop, Copy to clipboard, and PNG / PDF export built in. The markup tools — blur, arrows, shapes, and text — along with JPEG/WebP export and compression control remain Pro, shown right in the toolbar with a one-click upgrade. It’s the easiest way to mark up a screenshot and to see exactly what Pro adds, without leaving the page.

Fixed

  • Captures of modern web apps that scroll inside an inner panel — chat assistants, dashboards, and other single-page apps — are much cleaner. Toolbars and bars that float over the content no longer repeat down the image, empty side areas are filled with the page’s own background instead of showing as black bands, and elements no longer overlap or get cut off at scroll boundaries. Pages that fit on a single screen also capture exactly as shown, with nothing collapsing on top of itself.

Improved

  • A locked Pro tool in the editor now shows a clear, dismissible prompt explaining that one subscription unlocks the whole Pro toolset — so you can keep using the free tools or upgrade in a click, without interrupting your edit.

v1.2.0

May 2026

Added

  • Copy to clipboard — a new free-tier toggle in the popup (“Copy to clipboard”, off by default). When enabled, every capture is downloaded as usual and copied to your system clipboard as a PNG, ready to paste into Slack, email, or any image-aware app. The dedicated clipboard button in the Pro editor toolbar (next to Export) continues to work for one-click copy after editing.

Fixed

  • Page titles containing emoji or decorative symbols (★ ♥ ™ © flag glyphs) could produce screenshot filenames that wouldn’t save cleanly on Windows or appeared as garbled boxes when shared across platforms. These characters are now stripped from generated filenames, with any stranded hyphens trimmed, so saved files always have clean, portable names. Non-Latin alphabets (Japanese, Cyrillic, Arabic, accented Latin, CJK) continue to be preserved unchanged.
  • Blur regions in the editor faded toward the original image at high strength — the underlying screenshot started bleeding through the blur once the strength slider passed about 70, with the worst result at 100. Blur regions now stay fully opaque at every strength.

v1.1.0

May 2026

Added

  • Pro users can now choose the compression rate for screenshots. A new Compression setting in the popup picks one of four presets — Smaller files, Balanced, High quality, or Maximum quality — and applies to JPEG, WebP, and PDF captures. Default is High quality, matching previous behavior.
  • The built-in editor gains a Compression button next to the format selector, opening a panel with the same presets plus a fine-tune quality slider and a live estimate of the resulting file size — so you can dial in the size/quality trade-off per export instead of committing globally.

Fixed

  • Users granted Pro access on the billing dashboard could stay locked out for up to a week if they confirmed the login link on a different browser or device. The next time the popup opens after Pro is granted, the upgrade now unlocks promptly.
  • Pro users who had “Open in Editor” turned on still saw their captures download as files instead of opening in the editor. The setting now applies as expected, so captures land in the editor when the toggle is on.
  • Captures of pages with pop-up dialogs, lightboxes, or small floating buttons (such as “Book Now” or “Back to top”) sometimes placed those elements in awkward positions in the final image, leaving an empty band running through the middle of the page. Captures now skip these floating elements cleanly, so the underlying page comes through as expected. Sticky headers, footers, and tall narrow side rails continue to capture normally.
  • Pages with videos — hero videos, product demos, looping background reels — showed a visible split or mismatched halves in captures where a video was on screen. Videos are now held still during capture and resume normally afterward, so the final image looks continuous.
  • Pages with wide layouts that don’t fully fit your browser window — fixed-width navigation bars, oversized hero sections, dashboard-style headers — were sometimes captured with the right edge cut off. Rendry now scales the page to fit during capture, so the full design appears in the final image.

Improved

  • Editor wheel-zoom (Ctrl/Cmd + scroll) now zooms toward the cursor instead of the viewport center, so the point under your pointer stays put as you zoom in and out — matching the feel of Figma, Photoshop, and other image editors.
  • The “Unlock Pro Features” panel now appears at the top of the popup instead of the bottom, so it’s immediately visible when you open it from the header link, a locked Pro control, or a Pro-only format button.
  • Editor toolbar now wraps onto additional rows when the browser window is narrow, instead of letting controls overlap each other. The toolbar stays a single row at normal widths and grows vertically only when needed.
