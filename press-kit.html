One-liner

One-click full-page screenshots — private, clean, and fast.

Short pitch

Rendry captures entire web pages as PNG, JPEG, or PDF for free. Smart Redact detects sensitive details locally; a no-card Pro trial blurs them for review before sharing.

Long pitch

Rendry is a full-page screenshot Chrome extension for QA and support teams that share browser evidence. PNG, JPEG, and PDF capture is free, with a built-in editor for cropping and copying. Smart Redact detects potential sensitive details on-device in a free preview that is clearly marked unblurred. A no-card 7-day Pro trial blurs those detections on the same capture for review before export. Pro is $2/month or $20/year and also adds WebP, extraction, element capture, and markup tools. Captures stay on the user's device; ExtensionPay handles license verification and billing.

Key facts

  • Price: Free. Optional Pro at $2/month or $20/year.
  • Platform: Chrome extension, Manifest V3.
  • Publisher: MamaOwned LLC (United States).
  • Privacy: Captures and page content stay on-device. No analytics or screenshot uploads. ExtensionPay handles Pro license verification and billing.
  • Permissions: activeTab, scripting, downloads, storage, offscreen, clipboardWrite. No broad all-sites host permission.

Differentiators

  • PDF export is free. Most competitors gate PDF behind a paywall.
  • No account for the free tier. Install and capture, no email required.
  • Screenshots never leave the user's machine. All capture and editing is on-device — free tier and Pro alike.
  • Noise removal is automatic. Cookie banners and chat widgets hidden before the shot.
